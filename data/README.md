# Data

| File | What it holds | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ctx_uxo_crops_64.zip`, `crops_manifest.csv`, `photos_manifest.csv` | 15,449 crops of 64 × 64 pixels, one per labelled object, with their split, category and source photo, as prepared in the author's P4 | CTX-UXO (Craioveanu and Stamatescu, National University of Science and Technology Politehnica Bucharest), [Zenodo record 17052675](https://zenodo.org/records/17052675) | CC BY 4.0 |
| `background_crops_64.zip`, `background_manifest.csv` | 300 crops of ground with no item, a fixed sample (seed 0) of the US-site background crops prepared in the author's P5 | SULAND (Vivoli et al., 2024, University of Florence), with the SULAND_v2 labels (Lekhak et al., Rochester Institute of Technology) | CC BY 4.0 |
| `generated_crops_64.zip`, `generated_manifest.csv` | 100 images generated by P5's GAN, retrained here (`triage/generated_images.py`); every one is marked as generated | Generated from SULAND crops | CC BY 4.0 (derived) |
| `ch_municipalities_degurba.csv` | Every Swiss municipality: population (2024), area, density and degree of urbanisation (1 city, 2 town or suburb, 3 rural area) | Eurostat, [LAU correspondence table "EU-27-LAU-2025-NUTS-2024"](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/nuts/local-administrative-units), sheet CH, downloaded on 8 November 2026 | Reuse authorised provided the source is acknowledged ([Eurostat copyright notice](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/help/copyright-notice)) |

Changes made to the Eurostat table: only the Swiss sheet is kept, the columns are renamed, the area is converted to
km², and the density is computed from the population and the area. Eurostat is not responsible for this extract.

The SHA-256 of every file is checked in code before use (`triage/photo_reader.py`, `triage/generated_images.py`, `triage/municipalities.py`). The files are kept byte for byte (`data/** -text` in `.gitattributes`), so a checkout on Windows does not change their line endings.
